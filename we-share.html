Features

Built for
Real Speed

Every component engineered to push files from device to device at the physical limits of your local network — no cloud, no accounts, no compromise.

01 —
📡
Smart Discovery

Dual-mode scanning via Bluetooth LE and UDP radar. Finds devices instantly — no IP addresses, no configuration.

02 —
🔗
QR Handshake

Cross-network transfers via QR scan fallback when auto-discovery can't reach across subnets.

03 —
⚡
Turbo TCP

Multi-threaded socket engine saturates your LAN bandwidth with real-time progress.

04 —
📂
File Queue

Add, remove, reorder batches before sending. Full control over every transfer session.

05 —
🔒
Secure by Design

100% local. Files never touch a server. Built-in handshake verification ensures only authorized devices receive.

06 —
🌐
Cross-Platform

PC↔PC, Phone↔Phone, PC↔Phone. Native on Windows and Android from one codebase.
