Features

Everything you need to
move files fast.

No throttling, no cloud handoff, no accounts. We Share stays out of your way.

Turbo Transfer

A multi-threaded engine that maxes out your local connection. Transfer a 10 GB folder in seconds on Gigabit LAN — no throttling, no upload queues.

Full Local Speed

Auto Discovery

Devices appear on the radar automatically. No IPs, no pairing, no setup — just open and share.

Web Portal

Any phone or tablet on the same Wi-Fi can share via browser at :8080. No app needed.

Accept / Reject

Every incoming transfer asks for permission. You decide what lands on your machine — full control, every time.

Transfer History

Every transfer is logged locally — file names, sizes, devices, timestamps. Always on your machine, never in the cloud.

End-to-End Local & Private

Files travel directly between devices on your network. No servers, no cloud relays, no accounts, no data collection. The source code is open and verifiable.

Zero Cloud
How It Works

Up and running in
under a minute.

01

Open on both devices

Launch We Share on the sender and receiver. Devices appear on each other's radar within seconds — completely automatic.

02

Pick files, pick target

Drag and drop any files into We Share. Select the destination device from the radar. Hit Send.

03

Accept & receive

The receiver taps Accept. Transfer begins at full local speed. No clouds, no wait, no internet required.

Desert Mode

No router?
Not a problem.

When there's no Wi-Fi at all, We Share creates its own hotspot and silently connects other PCs to it. No passwords typed, no settings changed — fully automatic from open to transfer.

01 PC A opens We Share → auto-creates "WeShare" hotspot
02 PC B opens We Share → detects hotspot, connects silently — no password
03 Both appear on each other's radar → transfer as normal
