How We Share Works in 3 Steps

Zero configuration. No passwords to remember. Instant transfers.

01

Open We Share

Launch the app on both computers. Devices on the same local network automatically detect each other on the radar.

02

Select Files & Recipient

Drag and drop photos, 4K videos, documents, or entire folders. Tap the destination device on the radar to send.

03

One-Click Accept

The receiver reviews the file manifest and taps Accept. The transfer streams directly over local TCP sockets at wire speed.
