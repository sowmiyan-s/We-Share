The Problem

Moving files between PCs shouldn't be this hard

Your PCs are 3 feet apart on your desk. Your files are the same size as always. Yet somehow, getting them from one screen to the other has become a 20-minute exercise in frustration. There's got to be a better way.

☁
Cloud uploads are painfully slow

Upload 4 GB to Google Drive, wait 20 minutes, then download again. Every byte travels across the internet and back — for a file that's 3 feet away.

01
⊞
USB cables are messy and limited

Find the right cable. Plug in. Wait for Windows to recognise the drive. Hope the file fits. Unplug. Repeat. It's 2026 — we shouldn't need cables.

02
◇
Email and messaging apps cap file sizes

25 MB attachment limit. Compressed to death. Split into parts. WhatsApp destroys photo quality. Telegram is 2 GB max. Nothing cuts it for large files.

03

Blazing Fast Transfers

We Share moves files at the full speed of your local network — up to 150 MB/s. That 4 GB video? Done in under 30 seconds. No upload bar. No waiting on a server.

◐
Your Files Stay Private

Files travel directly from one PC to another. They never leave your network. No cloud, no server ever sees your data. Total privacy by design, not just policy.

⊗
Finds Devices Automatically

Open the app — your other PCs appear instantly. No typing IP addresses. No configuration. No pairing codes. Just tap the device you want and start sending.

⊕
Works Without Internet

In a hotel? Offline at a job site? On an airplane? Doesn't matter. We Share only needs your local Wi-Fi — not the internet. Any network, anywhere on earth.

No File Size Restrictions

Send a 1 KB text file or a 500 GB archive — We Share doesn't care. No splitting. No compression. No "file too large" errors. Your files, unmodified, instantly.
