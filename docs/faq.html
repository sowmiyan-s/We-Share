Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about We Share. Can't find your answer? Open an issue on GitHub.

General
We Share is a free, open-source desktop app for Windows that lets you transfer files between devices at full local network speed — with no internet, no cloud, and no accounts required. It works on Wi-Fi, LAN, or even with no router at all (Desert Mode).
Yes — completely. We Share is MIT licensed, 100% open source, and free forever. No subscriptions, no premium tiers, no ads.
No. We Share is designed to work entirely on your local network — or even with no network at all (using Desert Mode). Your files never leave your local environment.
Absolutely not. No account, no sign-in, no email. Just install, open, and transfer.
Compatibility
The desktop app runs on Windows 10 (build 19041+) and Windows 11. However, any device — iPhone, Android, Mac, Linux — can receive and send files using the built-in Web Portal via a browser.
Yes! We Share includes a built-in Web Portal. Open http://<pc-ip>:8080 in your phone's browser (same Wi-Fi required) and you can upload files from your phone to the PC, or download files from the PC — no app install needed on the phone.
No limits on file types or file sizes. Transfer any file — videos, ISO images, folders, documents, anything. The only limit is your network speed and storage space.
Features
Desert Mode is We Share's automatic hotspot feature. When no Wi-Fi router is detected, one PC automatically creates a Wi-Fi hotspot named WeShare. The other PC silently detects and connects to it — no passwords, no manual steps. Then the devices appear on each other's radar and you transfer normally.
We Share broadcasts a UDP signal on the local network. Nearby devices running We Share hear the broadcast and appear on the radar automatically. No manual IP entry, no pairing codes, no configuration.
Yes. Every incoming file transfer shows a notification with the sender's name, file name(s), and size. You can Accept or Reject each transfer individually. Nothing is saved without your approval.
Yes. A local SQLite database tracks your transfer history including file names, sizes, sender/receiver, and timestamps. This database stays on your machine and is never uploaded anywhere.
Privacy & Security
Completely. We Share is local-only — your files travel directly from one device to another on your network. Nothing is sent to any server, no telemetry, no analytics, no data collection of any kind. The source code is open for anyone to verify.
Yes, completely normal. We Share uses local TCP and UDP sockets for device discovery and file transfer. Windows Firewall needs to allow this traffic for the app to function. The installer automatically adds the required rules. The rules are specific to the We Share executable and are removed when you uninstall.
Troubleshooting
  • ▸ Make sure both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network or LAN.
  • ▸ Check that Windows Firewall isn't blocking We Share (the installer adds rules, but check manually if needed).
  • ▸ If on a public Wi-Fi or corporate network, device isolation may be enabled — try a home network.
  • ▸ Restart We Share on both devices.
  • ▸ If no router is available, use Desert Mode — open We Share on both PCs and it will self-connect.
We Share uses all available bandwidth — the transfer speed is limited by your network hardware. For fastest speeds: use a wired Ethernet connection if possible, or Wi-Fi 5/6. Speeds of 500 MB/s+ are achievable on Gigabit LAN.
Download the latest installer from the Downloads page and run it. The installer will detect the previous version and offer a clean upgrade while keeping your transfer history intact.
