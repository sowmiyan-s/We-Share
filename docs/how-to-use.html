How to Share Anything with We Share

Easy, step-by-step instructions for sharing files between laptops, sending to iPhone or Android phones using your camera, and sharing completely offline.

Sharing Between Two Computers

The fastest way to move movies, photos, and large folders between two Windows laptops or desktops on the same local network.

1

Open We Share on both computers

Start the app on both computers. Devices on the same Wi-Fi or wired network automatically detect each other in seconds.

2

Pick the files you want to send

Click the circular Send button and choose your files, or simply drag and drop files and folders right into the window.

3

Select recipient on the Radar

Discovered devices orbit the radar scanner. Click the name or icon of the computer you want to send files to.

4

The receiver clicks Accept

The receiver sees an instant prompt with your computer's name and file metrics. As soon as they click Accept, files stream directly across at wire speed.

Zero Internet Used: Transfers stream directly through your local router via raw TCP sockets. Even if your ISP connection is down, transfers work at full hardware speeds with zero cellular data.

Sending Files to iPhone or Android (Web Transfer)

Send photos, videos, or documents from your computer directly to any phone. The phone does not need any app installed!

1

Open Web Transfer on your PC

Ensure your computer and phone are connected to the same Wi-Fi network.

2

Scan the QR code with phone camera

Point your iPhone or Android camera at the QR code displayed on your PC screen. Tap the notification pill to open the local session in Safari, Chrome, or Firefox.

3

Select the device and send files

On your PC, select the phone card from the Web Transfer panel to open the dedicated Device Session. Click Choose Files to Send to push files directly to the mobile browser.

4

Save directly to your phone

The files appear on the phone's browser view. Tap to download them straight to your camera roll or Downloads folder.

Universal Compatibility: Works seamlessly with Apple iPhones, iPads, Android smartphones, and tablets without requiring app downloads from the App Store or Google Play.

Uploading Photos & Files from Phone to Computer

Copy high-resolution photos and 4K videos from your smartphone over to your PC without cables or cloud subscription limits.

1

Open the Web Portal on your phone

Scan the PC's QR code to open the mobile upload dashboard.

2

Select photos or files on your phone

Tap Choose Files on the phone screen to select media from your photo library or document storage.

3

Approve the transfer on your PC

Your PC displays the incoming file notification with filename and size metrics. Click Accept to permit the transfer.

4

Files arrive directly in Downloads

The files stream directly over your local network and are safely stored in your designated Downloads folder.

Sharing Anywhere Without Wi-Fi (Desert Mode)

Transfer files when traveling on a train, plane, in a vehicle, or outdoors with no Wi-Fi router available.

1

Click Start Desert Hotspot on Laptop A

When outside Wi-Fi range, click Start Desert Hotspot. We Share automatically configures an ad-hoc local Wi-Fi station.

2

Open We Share on Laptop B

The second laptop detects the "WeShare" ad-hoc network and connects silently via native Windows APIs without password prompts.

3

Share files immediately

Both laptops appear on each other's radar. Pick your files, tap Send, and transfer at full wireless speed anywhere in the world.

Seamless Restoration: When you stop Desert Mode or close We Share, your computer automatically reverts to its standard Wi-Fi network with zero residual settings modified.
