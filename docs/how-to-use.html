How to Use

Share files in any scenario

Step-by-step guides for every way you'd want to share files — PC to PC, phone to PC, PC to phone, and even with no Wi-Fi at all.

PC → PC PC → Mobile Mobile → PC Desert Mode

PC to PC

Both PCs must be on the same Wi-Fi or LAN network. We Share auto-discovers them — no setup needed.

1

Install & open We Share on both PCs

Download and run the installer on both machines. Launch We Share — it will automatically scan your network and display nearby devices on the radar.

2

Select your files on the sender

On the PC you want to send from, click the send area or drag and drop your files into We Share. You can send multiple files at once.

3

Pick the destination PC from the radar

The radar shows all discovered devices. Click the target PC's name or icon to select it as your destination.

4

Click Send

Hit the Send button. The receiver's PC will show a notification popup with the incoming file details.

5

Accept on the receiving PC

The receiver clicks Accept on the notification. The transfer starts immediately at full local network speed. Done!

Tip: Transfer speed depends on your local network. On a Gigabit LAN or fast Wi-Fi 6, you can expect hundreds of MB/s.

PC to Mobile (Web Portal)

Your phone doesn't need the We Share app at all. Use the built-in Web Portal from any mobile browser.

1

Open We Share on your PC

Launch We Share. The Web Portal starts automatically in the background on port 8080.

2

Connect your phone to the same Wi-Fi

Make sure your phone is on the same Wi-Fi network as the PC running We Share.

3

Find the PC's local IP address

In We Share, the Web Portal address is shown in the app. It looks like this:

http://192.168.x.x:8080
4

Open that address in your phone's browser

Type the address into Chrome, Safari, or any browser on your phone. The We Share Web Portal loads instantly — no install.

5

Browse and download files

From the web portal you can browse files that the PC is sharing, download them to your phone, or upload files from your phone to the PC.

Works on any device with a browser — iPhone, Android, iPad, even another PC. No app needed on the receiving side.

Mobile to PC (Upload via Web Portal)

Send files from your phone to the PC using the same built-in Web Portal — no cables, no apps to install on the phone.

1

Open We Share on your PC & note the portal address

Launch We Share on the PC. The Web Portal address (e.g. http://192.168.1.5:8080) is shown in the app interface.

2

Open the portal address on your phone

Join the same Wi-Fi and open the address in your phone's browser. The We Share portal appears.

3

Tap Upload & pick your files

Use the Upload button in the Web Portal to select photos, videos, documents, or any file from your phone's storage.

4

Files land on your PC instantly

Uploaded files are saved directly to the PC's configured receive folder. No confirmation needed on the PC side.

Desert Mode — No Wi-Fi Needed

Transferring files when there's no router or Wi-Fi at all? We Share handles it automatically.

1

Open We Share on PC A (no Wi-Fi)

When We Share detects no network, it automatically creates a Wi-Fi hotspot named WeShare on this PC. You don't need to do anything.

2

Open We Share on PC B (no Wi-Fi)

PC B detects the WeShare hotspot and silently connects to it — no password prompt, no manual steps. The app handles everything.

3

Transfer as normal

Both PCs now see each other on the radar. Select files, pick the target, click Send, and Accept on the other side. Exactly the same as normal mode.

Fully automatic. No passwords to type, no settings to change, no extra software needed. We Share figures out the entire connection by itself.
